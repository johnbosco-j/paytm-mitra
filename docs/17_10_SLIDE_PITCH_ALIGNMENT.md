A truthful message that still wins attention: "We have implemented a working consent-based unmet-demand ledger, Tamil voice capture, margin-aware stocking trial and auditable approval/measurement loop. The architecture is prepared for future supplier, reconciliation and Paytm-approved integrations. Here's the user-driven action changing live." Adjust implemented claims to what has actually passed tests.
